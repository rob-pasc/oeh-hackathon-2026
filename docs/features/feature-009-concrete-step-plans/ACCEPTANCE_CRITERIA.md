## Review and integration evidence

Completion boundary: locally verified handoff with self-review ([plan](./PLAN.md#review-and-integration)). AC-015 needs a configured `LLM_API_KEY` and is a review of live output, not a guarantee.

**Self-review of the final diff, 2026-11-21** (commits 22938bd, bf1ed95 and the docs commit after them, against the approved scope in `PLAN.md`). Self-review is not independent approval.

- **Scope:** FR-001 to FR-013 are implemented; nothing outside the plan was added. The new configuration (`LLM_STEP_TIMEOUT_MS`, `LLM_THINKING_STEPS`, `STEPS_PER_HOUR`) and the `steps:smoke` script serve the approved requirements and are recorded in the plan.
- **Finding, fixed:** The live review showed the AI marking reassembly and compatibility checks as `general`, which bypasses FR-005's citation rule. The step-kind rule in the prompt now names them as technical and forbids marking a step general to avoid a citation (steps-v2), and `not_suitable` redirects may no longer name part specifications (steps-v3).
- **Finding, fixed:** Stale comments in `web/src/lib/categoryView.ts` and `web/src/lib/pathView.ts` still said step plans would arrive with feature 009; updated.
- **Finding, accepted:** The step-kind classification is prompt-only, as the plan's risks say. A general step can still carry a compatibility sentence without a citation (AC-015 residual finding).
- **Finding, accepted:** A route error (rate limit, server error) shows "The steps could not be loaded" in the card; reopening the card tries again. FR-011's template covers AI failures, not transport errors, so this is outside its scope.
- **Checked:** No secrets in the diff; `.env` files untouched except the two `.env.example` templates. The `step_cache` table is additive. Commits are on local `main`, not pushed.

## Verification notes

**Live demo review (AC-015), 2026-11-21.** `npm run steps:smoke -w server` makes the category call and then the step call for every option, on synthetic scans of the three demo models (`server/scripts/demo-scans.ts`; no real demo scans exist yet). It lists candidates for manual review: battery plans without a professional step, and general steps that name tools, parts or internal components. The category answers differ between runs, so each run covered different options.

- **Run 1 (steps-v1), 22 plans, all from the AI:** All three internal battery replacements were `professional`. Four technical steps were marked `general` and uncited: "Reconnect the battery and refit the base cover" (XPS 13 9360 SSD salvage) and three compatibility checks that named part specifications. This fails the "no disguised technical step" check, so the prompt was changed.
- **Run 2 (steps-v2), 26 plans:** Reassembly and compatibility checks came back `technical`, with citations. Two redirect steps in `not_suitable` options still named part specifications ("8 GB DDR4-2400 SO-DIMM") without a citation, so the `not_suitable` rule now asks for the other option by name only.
- **Run 3 (steps-v3), 24 plans:** Every internal battery replacement was `professional`, and plans named the Geräte-Retter-Prämie and repair cafés where passages supported them. One borderline step remains: the T480 salvage plan's general step "Confirm the whole laptop is no longer usable before salvaging" ends with an uncited compatibility sentence. This run had no `not_suitable` options, so the steps-v3 rule was not exercised live.
- **Latency:** step calls took 1.9 to 8.7 s, well within the 25 s deadline.
