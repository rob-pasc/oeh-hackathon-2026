# Dell XPS 13 9360 SSD Replacement (iFixit guide 170743)

## Introduction

If you need to replace or upgrade the SSD for your Dell XPS 13 9360 laptop, follow this guide.

Replacing the SSD on this model could help improve its storage space and performance. The model shown in this guide contains a 256 GB M.2 PCIe NVMe SSD, however, the model can upgrade to a 1 TB SSD.

Before you begin this repair, power off your computer and disconnect it from the charger. In addition, make sure to disconnect the battery before replacing the SSD. This will help prevent accidental damage to the computer or its electrical components.

Difficulty: Moderate
Time required: 20 - 35 minutes

## Step 3

Use a Torx T5 screwdriver to remove eight 3 mm screws around the edge of the back cover.

## Step 4

Lift the small magnetic flap cover in the center of the back case.
Use a JIS J0 screwdriver to remove the 6 mm screw under the flap cover.

## Step 6

Disconnect the battery before removing the SSD.
Use the JIS J0 screwdriver to remove the single 2 mm screw securing the existing SSD.
The SSD will automatically "pop up".
Gently slide it out from the socket.

## Step 7

Slide the new SSD into the slot at the same angle used when removing the old one.
Make sure to align the notches on the SSD correctly with the tab openings of the slot to avoid damaging the SSD and the PCIe slot.
