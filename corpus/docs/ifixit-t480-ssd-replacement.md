# Lenovo ThinkPad T480 SSD Replacement (iFixit guide 167229)

Author: Dane Elliott-Dyson and 3 other contributors (iFixit). Difficulty: Moderate. Time required: 10 - 15 minutes. Community-contributed guide, last updated Aug 28, 2024.

## Introduction

This guide will show you how to replace the SSD on your Lenovo Thinkpad T480 laptop. Backup your data before replacing if possible.

## Step 1 SSD

Disable the battery, and unplug your device before you begin. Flip laptop over on its back.

[The guide links "Disable the battery" to the Lenovo ThinkPad T480 User Guide, page 118.]

## Step 2

Push Clip 1 to the side. While holding Clip 2 to the side, push the battery out of the laptop.

## Step 3

Remove all Phillip-head screws shown.

The screws will not come out of the casing, so unscrew them all the way until they don't go any higher.

## Step 4

Pry up on the casing using a spudger or a flat-head screwdriver and remove the back.

## Step 5

Pry up on the little metal bar on the connector and pull the connector out of its socket.

## Step 6

Pry up on the left side of the SSD casing. Pull the casing to the left, and remove.

## Step 7

Remove the following 4mm Phillip-head screws and remove the plate.

## Step 8

Remove the 4mm Phillip-head screw and pull the SSD out of its socket.

## Conclusion

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
