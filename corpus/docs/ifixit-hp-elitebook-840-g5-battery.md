# HP EliteBook 840 G5 Battery Replacement (iFixit guide 109791, by Ryan McMullen)

## Introduction

Difficulty: Easy. Time required: 5 - 10 minutes.

***Warning:*** There may be multiple iterations of this laptop, and each uses a different battery model. Before you purchase a replacement battery, open the back cover up to visually confirm which battery replacement you should purchase.

As this replacement requires the backing to be completely removed, use caution during the replacement as the electrical components inside of your laptop will be exposed. Also ensure to carefully remove any connecting wires by the heads-not the wires themselves. This will prevent any wires from breaking during the replacement.

## Tools

Phillips #1 Screwdriver; iFixit Opening Tool; TR9 Torx Security Screwdriver

## Parts

HP EliteBook 830, 840, 735, and 745 G5 Battery; HP EliteBook 830, 840, 735, and 745 G5 Battery - SS03XL - Genuine

## Back Cover

Use a Phillips #1 screwdriver to undo the five screws. The top three screws are 10 mm and the two side screws are 7 mm. Unscrew the bottom three screws using the same screwdriver. The bottom Phillips #1 screws don't remove from the back plate, but do need to be unscrewed.

Use the plastic opening tool to pry open the back plate. [...] Grab the back plate and separate it slowly away from the device. No wiring is connected to the back plate. Separate with caution, as there are electric components on the inside.

## Battery

Remove the 4mm screws that are fastened on the battery with your T9 Torx screwdriver.

Carefully remove the power supply connector by tugging it from the sides of its head. Make sure not to pull on the wires, as they could become damaged.

Slide the battery out and remove it from the device.

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
