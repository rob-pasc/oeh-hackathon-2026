# Lenovo devices supported for upgrade to Windows 11 - Lenovo Support US

## Introduction

Systems listed below will be supported for upgrade from Windows 10 to Windows 11.

If your device does not meet the stated system requirements, you may not be able to install Windows 11 on your device. Check for compatibility using the Microsoft PC Health Check App.

## Before Upgrading

Before upgrading from Window 10 to Windows 11, we recommend the following steps:
Back up your files. By default, your files and data will transfer to your new PC.
Make a backup of the Bitlocker key (Finding your BitLocker recovery key in Windows).

## After Upgrading

After upgrading to Windows 11, update drivers, applications, and software.

If your device is not listed in the table below, an upgrade path may not be available.
